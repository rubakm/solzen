Students can know a concept and still not understand it

Many learners find it hard to picture an idea, hold it together as a whole, and then use it to solve a problem they have not seen before.

Memorising the steps can pass a test. It rarely leaves a student ready to apply what they know. SOLZEN exists to close that gap.

Our mission: Make learning deeper, measurable and applicable.

The SOLZEN framework

Understand

Assess

Reinforce

Apply

Start from the concept, not the answer. Learners see how an idea is built before they are asked to recall it.

Check what has actually been understood, so the next step responds to evidence instead of assumption.

Return to the gaps with practice shaped to the learner, in the language and context they know.

Carry the idea into problems and situations that look like the real world.

  1. Understand
  2. Assess
  3. Reinforce
  4. Apply

How we think

Concept-first
Understanding comes before memorisation.
Human-centred
Explanations follow how learners naturally think.
Visual
Difficult ideas become easier to see, connect and explore.
Measurable
Evidence of learning decides what comes next.
Applicable
Learning leads to confident problem-solving and action.

Technology in service of learning

We look to visualisation, interactive learning, assessment, personalised reinforcement and regional, localised learning to make the framework work for more students.

Technology is a means. The aim is understanding. How we build it will be shared when we are ready to announce it.

The future of learning should not be about consuming more information. It should be about understanding better.
